# Check your installation

Choose a system and complete its runbook, then follow its self-check instructions:

| System | Instructions |
|---|---|
| RAD | [Fixture, native sample and checkpoint checks](https://github.com/Kibnelson/rad_tyg1/blob/rad/CHECKS.md) |
| Milvus | [Source, services and native sample checks](https://github.com/Kibnelson/rad_tyg1/blob/milvus/CHECKS.md) |
| SIMD-DPK | [Fixture and full-reference sample checks](https://github.com/Kibnelson/rad_tyg1/blob/simd_dpk/CHECKS.md) |
| Faiss(Jaccard) | [Fixture and native sample checks](https://github.com/Kibnelson/rad_tyg1/blob/faiss_jaccard/CHECKS.md) |

These pages describe checks to run on your own machine. Generated outputs belong
in your local `results/` directory. CPU requirements are in [MACHINE_SPECS.md](MACHINE_SPECS.md).
